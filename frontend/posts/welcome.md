# Welcome to Neura — rigorous results, made simple

Neura turns your research data into a **publication-ready Results section** — and now also runs **meta-analysis**, **sample-size calculations**, and **diagnostic-accuracy** analyses, each delivered as a clear, cited report you can download as Word and PDF.

## How it works

1. **Upload** your protocol and data (Excel or CSV — Arabic is supported).
2. The AI **proposes** the right statistical test, and **you approve or edit it** — this human checkpoint is the heart of Neura.
3. Your analysis runs in a secure sandbox, and the AI **writes up the results** grounded strictly in the numbers.
4. **Download** the Word and PDF, formatted to match your thesis or a journal style.

## Why researchers trust it

- **Nothing is invented.** Every statistic comes from the actual analysis, and every method cites a real reference.
- **You stay in control.** The AI suggests; you decide. An analyst chat lets you ask questions, change the test, or add analyses in plain language.
- **English or Arabic**, with right-to-left output for Arabic write-ups.
