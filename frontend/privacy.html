Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Template — have a professional review before you rely on it.

What we collect

Your account email; the study protocol and data files you upload; and the outputs generated for you (statistical results and the Results section). Payment is handled by EasyKash — we receive confirmation of payment but do not store your card details.

How we use it

Your inputs are used solely to run your requested analysis and produce your Results section. A summary of your data (column names, types) is sent to our AI provider to propose a statistical plan and write the results; we do not sell your data.

Storage & retention

Inputs and outputs are stored to deliver your task and are automatically deleted 30 days after you accept the results. You can request earlier deletion.

Third parties

We use an AI model provider to generate the plan and text, and EasyKash to process payments. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service.

Your rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time via your support contact.
